GOVERNMENT SUPPORT

Schemes for Micro Businesses

Explore official programmes before you prepare a loan application. Final approval is decided by the scheme authority and lender.

PMEGP

Prime Minister Employment Generation Programme

Margin-money subsidy for eligible new manufacturing and service/business micro-enterprises.

Key eligibility

  • Individual applicant must be 18 or older; no income ceiling.
  • Only new projects; units already receiving another government subsidy are not eligible.
  • Class VIII pass required above ₹10 lakh manufacturing or ₹5 lakh service/business project cost.
Check PMEGP eligibility ↗
MUDRA

Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana

Collateral-free institutional credit for non-farm, income-generating micro-enterprises, including allied activities such as dairy and poultry.

Key eligibility

  • Business must need a term loan or working capital for an eligible activity.
  • Shishu: up to ₹50,000; Kishore: ₹50,000–₹5 lakh; Tarun: ₹5–₹10 lakh.
  • Tarun Plus: ₹10–₹20 lakh only after successful repayment of a prior Tarun loan.
View MUDRA details ↗
STAND-UP

Stand-Up India

Bank-loan support for setting up a new greenfield enterprise in manufacturing, services, trading, or allied agriculture.

Key eligibility

  • Women entrepreneurs or entrepreneurs from Scheduled Castes / Scheduled Tribes.
  • New (greenfield) enterprise; loan range ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore.
  • For a non-individual enterprise, eligible promoters must hold at least 51% ownership and control.
Explore Stand-Up India ↗
CGTMSE

Credit Guarantee Scheme (CGTMSE)

A government-backed credit guarantee that helps eligible lenders extend collateral-free credit to micro and small enterprises.

Key eligibility

  • New and existing viable micro or small enterprises may be covered through an eligible lender.
  • Apply to a member bank or financial institution; CGTMSE does not directly grant loans.
  • Credit must meet the lender’s and CGTMSE’s current eligibility conditions.
